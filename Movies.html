Movie Log

1. Marriage Story

I have watched this movie at least four times and even with the 2 hour 16 min run time it feels so quick. This movie changes the more I have watched it. At first enjoying the performances and the lavious, although somewhat lonely, locations these charecters occupy. On my most recent watch through I empathise more with the charecters feeling their emotions more clearly then I had before. I have never been married but now being with someone I love I can understand the pain it would feel like to lose someone like that.

10/10
